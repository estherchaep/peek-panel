A panel that remembers where you came from

peek-panel opens content over your page while keeping a slim strip of the page in view, so going back always feels one step away.

  • PeekHover the strip and the panel slides aside to reveal the page behind.
  • Many ways outClick or tap the strip, drag the edge, press Esc, or use any Back button.
  • Natural motionSprings that slide in and out, with Time and Bounce you can tune.
  • Phone layoutOn small screens the strip moves to the top and the panel slides up.
  • AccessibleFocus moves in and stays inside, then returns where you were. Reduced motion is respected.
  • Drop-inOne tag that works in plain HTML and any framework, with no dependencies.

Try it: hover the strip beside this panel, then click it, or press Esc.
